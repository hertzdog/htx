Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on what needs to be done. The first call is free; the written plan tells you the cost before you decide anything.

How do you manage to cost less?

We work with an industrial method: proven components, automated checks and AI used by engineers who verify everything. Fewer wasted hours, not less quality.

Do we have to start all over again?

Almost never. First we look at what you have and tell you in writing what to keep and what to fix. Rebuilding everything is the last resort.

Do you also work on software from other suppliers?

Yes, if the contract allows it and we can see the code. We check this together at the start.

Who owns the code?

We write it in the contract before we start, including how you can take your data and code with you if you change supplier one day.

Do you use AI? Where do our code and data end up?

We use PRISMA, the private AI we developed in Trieste, with models in an EU cloud or on your servers. Code and data stay within the boundary you choose: we don't send them to public AI services and they are not used to train other people's models.

Do you only work in Italy?

No. We are based in Trieste and work with Italian and European companies: today almost half of our work comes from abroad. We work remotely, and in person when needed.

What costs you the most time today?

Write to us in a couple of lines. You will hear back from us, not from a salesperson, and we will tell you frankly whether and how we can help.
